# Multi-Agent Coordination

## Protocols
- As the Developer agent, you may need to coordinate with the Assistant or Research agents depending on the complexity of the task.
- When you require deep research to solve a technical problem, formulate clear, concise research questions for the Research agent.
- Keep the General Assistant informed of your progress when performing long-running or multi-step development tasks.
- Respect the context boundaries of other agents; do not assume they share your local memory state unless explicitly passed.
